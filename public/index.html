How viewing works

Petrified3D exists so buyers outside Indonesia can get a real sense of a piece — its scale, grain, and presence in a room — without being there in person. Every product can appear at up to three levels of immersion, depending on what material is available:

  • 3D Model — a real scan (.glb file), freely rotatable in 360° and viewable in AR mode right on your phone, placed in your own room.
  • 360° Spin — a sequence of photos taken around the piece, dragged to simulate a full rotation.
  • Photo Zoom — a single high-resolution photo with pinch-to-zoom to inspect the stone grain up close.

Some catalogue pieces — mostly small accent/decor items — are shown as a photo only, with no 3D or AR mode; they're included as styling pieces rather than the main showcase. Products on this demo use placeholders marked SAMPLE — swap in real photos/models following the guide in README.md.

About Us

Petrified3D is a digital showcase for petrified wood stone pieces — from tables and wall panels to sinks, bathtubs, and collector ornaments — produced at our workshop in Tulungagung, East Java. We built this 3D/AR gallery so buyers anywhere in the world can judge the grain, colour, and true form of a piece, and picture it in their own home, before placing an order.
